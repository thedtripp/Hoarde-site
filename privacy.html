Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version: Hoarde does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

What Hoarde stores

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your focus sessions (start and end times, length, and whether they succeeded);
  • your hoard (coins and treasure you've earned);
  • the state of a session in progress, so it survives the app being closed.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it.

Screen lock detection

During a focus session, Hoarde checks whether your phone's screen is locked or unlocked. It uses standard operating-system signals for this, so it can tell locking your phone (allowed) apart from leaving the app (which ends the session). These signals are processed on your device and are not stored beyond the current session or shared. Hoarde cannot see which other apps you use.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Hoarde schedules them locally on your device. Examples are a reminder to come back to the app, and an alert when a session finishes. No notification content passes through a server.

Data we do not collect

Hoarde does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. It contains no third-party advertising or tracking.

Backups

Your device's own backup features (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup) may include Hoarde's data as part of your phone's backup. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We cannot access them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases

If Hoarde offers optional in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Before we add any service that processes purchase or diagnostic information, we will update this policy.

Children

Hoarde is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data the app collects, it will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@hoarde.app.
