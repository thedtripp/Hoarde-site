Frequently asked questions

What counts as leaving the app?

Switching to another app or going to your home screen while a session runs. You get a short grace period (10 seconds) for accidental swipes. Pulling down Control Center or the notification shade doesn't count.

Can I lock my phone during a session?

Yes, and you should! Locking your phone is the best way to use Hoarde. Your session keeps running and your coins are banked when the time is up.

I locked my phone but the goblin still got my loot. Why?

On iPhone, Hoarde detects locking most reliably when your phone has a passcode set. Without one, it relies on a quicker heuristic that occasionally misses. If this keeps happening, email us with your phone model and iOS version. It helps us improve detection.

Why does Hoarde want to send notifications?

So it can tell you when a session is finished, and warn you if you've left the app mid-session before the goblin strikes. Notifications are created on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

What happens if I give up?

You can cancel for free in the first 10 seconds. After that, giving up hands your stake to the goblin. Your vault and everything already in it are always safe.

Do I lose my hoard if I delete the app or change phones?

Your hoard is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup may restore it on a new phone. A built-in backup option is planned.

Does Hoarde track me or show ads?

No. There's no account, no ads and no analytics. See the privacy policy.
