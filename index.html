How it works

Every session puts a pile of coins (and sometimes a treasure) on the line.

Set the watch

Pick 10, 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes. Longer watches pay more per minute and roll for more treasure.

Lock it and leave it

Lock your phone and get to work. Locking is fine. Opening other apps is not.

Bank the loot

When time's up, coins pour into your vault with a satisfying clink, and any treasure is revealed.

Mind the goblin.

Leave Hoarde for more than a few seconds mid-session and the goblin sneaks in and runs off with your stake. Your vault itself is always safe. You only lose what you were guarding.

A small, honest consequence turns out to be very good at keeping your thumb off Instagram.
